Three Doors of Fate — Steam Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

The Steam edition of Three Doors of Fate collects no personal data through the game itself. It has no advertising, tracking, analytics, or in-app purchases, and the developer operates no game account server.

Local data

Game progress is stored on the player's computer in steam-progress-v1.json. It contains gameplay progress, unlocks, discoveries, achievement state, and a random installation identifier used only for deterministic save merging. The game does not send this file or identifier to a developer server.

Steam services

When enabled and available, Valve may process Steam account, achievement, and cloud data to operate the Steam client, Steam Achievements, and Steam Cloud. That processing is governed by Valve's services, agreements, account settings, and privacy controls. The complete single-player game and local saving remain available when those services are unavailable.

Support correspondence

If a player voluntarily contacts giha1205@gmail.com, the developer receives the sender address and information included in the message. It is used only to answer the request. Players should not send passwords, payment information, or Steam authentication codes.

Deletion

Players may remove local data from the operating system's game data folder after resolving Steam Cloud synchronization. Data hosted by Steam is managed with Valve's account and privacy controls. Support correspondence deletion requests can be sent to the support email above.
